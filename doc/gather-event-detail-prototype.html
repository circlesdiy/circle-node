D&B Mixing Workshop

📋 About this gathering

Join us for an intensive hands-on workshop where we'll dive deep into the fundamentals of mixing drum & bass. Whether you're just starting out or looking to refine your technique, this session will cover everything from crate digging and track selection to beatmatching and building energy throughout a set.

We'll be working with industry-standard equipment at Studio Complex's main room, with guidance from experienced DJs who've played at major festivals and club nights across the country.

What we'll cover:

• Track selection and reading the room
• Beatmatching techniques for fast-paced genres
• EQ and mixing fundamentals
• Building tension and energy in your sets
• Using effects tastefully
• Preparing for your first gig

What to bring:

• USB drive with your favorite D&B tracks
• Notebook for taking notes
• Your questions and enthusiasm!

The studio has professional CDJs and a mixer setup. We'll have time for everyone to practice, and there will be snacks and drinks provided throughout the afternoon.
